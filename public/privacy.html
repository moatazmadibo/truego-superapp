TrueGo Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-04-15

TrueGo by Truesoft Engineering is a Pi-powered mobility platform for demo ride-hailing and delivery workflows.

Information we collect

We may collect Pi username, Pi user ID, ride details, trip status data, driver assignment data, and Pi payment references needed to operate the service.

How we use information

We use collected information to authenticate users, dispatch rides, display ride history, process Pi payments, prevent duplicate payments, and improve service reliability.

Payments

Pi payments are processed through Pi Network tools. TrueGo stores only the necessary payment references such as payment ID, transaction ID, amount, and completion timestamp.

Data sharing

We do not sell user data. Information may be processed by infrastructure providers used to operate the application.
